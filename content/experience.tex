\definexp{phd}{University of Edinburgh}{Doctoral Researcher}{11/2023 -- present}
\xpdesc{phd}{industry}{%
  \begin{cvsub}
    \item Working on foundational aspects of neural network training dynamics in
          the context of high-dimensional model fitting to noisy experimental
          datasets. The theoretical framework based on the neural tangent kernel
          provides a quantitative explanation of the properties of neural
          network fits to experimental data, and tests whether their reported
          uncertainties are statistically faithful (\textit{Eur.Phys.J.C}).
    \item Member of the NNPDF collaboration, focusing on the
          extraction of parton distribution functions from global analysis of
          experimental data. Developed and maintained the production Python
          codebase used collaboration-wide. Extended the framework to account
          for theoretical uncertainties due to missing corrections in the
          perturbative expansion through a Bayesian analysis of the data,
          resulting in a new PDF set and now part of the future code
          release (\textit{EPJC}). Working in a collaborative environment with
          20+ international researchers, including group meetings,
          presentations, mentoring of new members and team working. Actively
          contributing to the forthcoming release of the NNPDF fitting codebase,
          NNPDF4.1.
    \item Working with external collaborators outside the NNPDF collaboration in
          the context of the MAP collaboration, extending the theoretical
          accuracy of the MAP inference framework to fragmentation functions
          (\href{https://github.com/MapCollaboration/MontBlanc/pull/10}{MontBlanc
          PR 10}). \end{cvsub}}
