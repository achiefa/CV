\cvsection{Research Interests}
\begin{cvlist}
  \item[] I am a Ph.D. candidate (2023--2027) working at the interface of QCD
          phenomenology and machine learning in the determination of parton
          distribution functions from experimental data. My research develops a
          quantitative understanding of how deep neural networks learn parton
          distributions: I use neural tangent kernel theory to characterise the
          training dynamics of PDF fits and to establish whether the
          uncertainties such fits report are faithful. I am an active member
          developer in the NNPDF collaborations, with six publications since
          2024 in \textit{PLB}, \textit{JHEP} and \textit{EPJC}.
\end{cvlist}
