\cvsection{Research Interests}
\begin{cvlist}
  \item[] I am a Ph.D. candidate (2023--2027) working at the interface of QCD phenomenology
          and machine learning. My research develops a quantitative understanding of how deep
          neural networks learn parton distributions: I use neural tangent kernel theory to
          characterise the training dynamics of PDF fits and to establish whether the
          uncertainties such fits report are faithful. I am an active developer in the NNPDF
          and MAP collaborations, with six publications since 2024 in \textit{PLB},
          \textit{JHEP} and \textit{EPJC}.
\end{cvlist}
